Career

From the microstructure to the organization.

  1. 2001 – 2007

    Technology Management, University of Stuttgart

    Mechanical engineering and business administration: thermal turbomachinery, computational fluid dynamics, materials science, strength of materials and finite element analysis.

  2. 2007

    Research Associate, Materials Testing Institute (MPA), University of Stuttgart

    Numerical and analytical component assessment in research and industry projects; teaching finite element analysis, materials science and strength of materials.

  3. 2009

    Head of Department, Welding and Joining Simulation & Laboratory, MPA

    Spot welding and friction stir welding, optimized through combined tests and simulation.

  4. 2011

    Doctorate (Dr.-Ing.)

    Evaluation of microstructural fatigue damage with stochastic methods

  5. 2011

    Team Leader, Computer-aided Modeling, Simulation and Optimization

    Continuum mechanics, simulation, design optimization and mathematical methods in product development.

  6. 2014

    Project Manager, Simulation and Optimization

    Modeling and optimization of electronic control units and mechatronic systems.

  7. 2016

    Senior Expert for Design, Simulation and Reliability; Project Manager SPDM

    Coordinated international simulation and reliability work for braking control, camera and radar systems, and led the industrialization of a simulation process management solution.

  8. 2020

    Senior Strategy Consultant, Digital Transformation and Data Integration

    Programs to digitalize key engineering processes as part of a data-driven end-to-end strategy.

  9. 2022

    Senior Manager, Mechanics Development Environment

    Led international teams providing CAD/PLM (Creo, Windchill) and CAE solutions of all kinds to about 1,500 engineers, and coordinated company-wide programs for design, simulation, data integration and digital twins.

  10. Since 2025

    Senior Project Manager, Digital Engineering

    Establishing a consistent digital thread in engineering by harmonizing processes, methods, data and toolsets, addressing basic ERP, PLM, ALM and data layer capabilities.

Languages German (native) · English (fluent) · Portuguese (good) · French (basic)
