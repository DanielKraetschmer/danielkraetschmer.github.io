How we get there

AI-assisted CAD & FEM

AI agents work in a sealed environment, script every step and check their own results.

  2. Model is meshed and solved
  3. Result is compared with a known answer

Simulation process & data management

We make it traceable.

Knowledge graphs connect simulation to the systems where your decisions are made.

  1. Requirement and product data are linked
  2. Geometry, mesh and run are recorded
  3. The decision keeps its full history

PCB thermal simulation

We read the board as built.

Gerber and drill files become a thermal model, without anyone drawing a CAD model.

  3. Results laid over the board layout
