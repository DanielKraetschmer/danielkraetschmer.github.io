Capability · Electronics thermal

From Gerber files to a live thermal model.

The board as manufactured becomes a checked thermal model, reduced to milliseconds and coupled with the components on it.

  1. Board model: hot spots from the Gerber and drill files
  2. Reduced model: any load profile in milliseconds
  3. Coupled components: junction and pins of the chip
