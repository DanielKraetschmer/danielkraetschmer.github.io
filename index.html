Capability · Drawings to 3D models

Your 2D drawings are machine-readable now.

Until now, only 3D master models with PMI counted as machine-readable. Yet most product knowledge still sits in 2D drawings: dimensions, tolerances, materials and notes. AI reads them into parametric 3D models and structured data, and proves every value against the original.

Tested on third-party drawings

  • 22 of 22Dimensions read from a turned-part drawing and measured on the model: all match
  • 26 of 26On a second drawing, a valve housing
  • 5° vs 10.3°A conflict found in the valve housing drawing: the written angle contradicts its own dimensions

  1. 1 · Read

    Views, dimensions, tolerances

    The drawing is read like a designer reads it: views and sections, every dimension, tolerance class, material and note.

  2. 2 · Model

    A parametric 3D model

    Every value becomes a named parameter in a script. Change one number and the model and its drawing follow.

  3. 3 · Prove

    Drawn again and compared

    The model's own drawing is laid over the original and every dimension is measured. Conflicts in the source are reported, not hidden.

What this enables

Your drawing archive is a training set.

Most companies hold thousands of released drawings. Read at scale, they show the rules your designers already follow, and where drawings contradict each other. Those rules can then train agents that review new designs.

1 · Read every drawing

From archive to data

Each drawing becomes a model and a structured record of dimensions, tolerances, materials and notes, checked against the original.

2 · Derive the rules

Patterns across parts

Preferred radii, tolerance classes, standard undercuts and hole patterns emerge from the set, together with the exceptions.

3 · Put them to work

Agents that know your standards

New drawings are checked against your own rules before release, and agents propose standard features while designers work.

Reading, modelling and checking single drawings is shown above. Deriving rules across a whole archive is the next step, built with your drawings.
