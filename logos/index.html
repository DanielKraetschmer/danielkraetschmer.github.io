Brand exploration

Logo Ideas

Fourteen concepts drawn from the work itself: solver fields, meshes, knowledge graphs and the path to a decision. Each one is tested on dark and light backgrounds, in one colour, and at icon size.

  1. SPDM Tech

    1 Stress Ring

    A loaded hole with stress peaking at two points, the classic benchmark. It says: we know exactly where the risk sits.

  2. SPDM Tech

    2 Hot Element

    A finite element mesh with a single highlighted cell: one critical spot found in the whole model.

  3. SPDM Tech

    3 Knowledge Graph

    Connected data objects converging on one bright node, the decision they all support.

  4. SPDM Tech

    4 Lineage

    A trace through requirement, model and run that climbs to a result. Traceability as a single line.

  5. SPDM Tech

    5 S-Path

    The S of SPDM as a data path between two nodes, from raw simulation to its business context.

  6. SPDM Tech

    6 Half Section

    An engineering section drawing: half solid, half outline around the axis, joining CAD craft and precision.

  7. SPDM Tech

    7 Isolines

    Contour lines spreading from a source, like a temperature or stress field seen from above.

  8. SPDM Tech

    8 Verified Node Selected

    A check mark built from mesh nodes. Every answer comes checked, and it ends in a result.

  9. SPDM Tech

    9 Result Scale

    A solver colour legend rising to its peak value, which also reads as growth for a business audience.

  10. SPDM Tech

    10 Two Fields

    One hexagon, half solid data and half engineering mesh: both fields of work given equal weight.

  11. SPDM Tech

    11 Focus

    Measuring marks closing in on one point: from a large model down to the single answer that matters.

  12. SPDM Tech

    12 Mode Shape

    A vibration mode between two supports with its peak highlighted: physics in one confident line.

  13. spdm tech

    13 S-Point

    A lowercase s and a full stop. Quiet and typographic: the answer, stated and finished.

  14. SPDM Tech

    14 App Tile

    A solid tile with a single S. The strongest option at small sizes and in social feeds.
