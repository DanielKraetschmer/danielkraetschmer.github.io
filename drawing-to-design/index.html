How it works

Four steps, each one a script you can rerun.

  1. 1 · Read

    The drawing

    Views and sections, every dimension, tolerance class, material and note, as a designer reads them.

  2. 2 · Model

    Parametric 3D model and graph

    Every value becomes a named parameter and a node in the knowledge graph. Change one, and model and drawing follow.

  3. 3 · Mesh

    91,172 nodes

    Second-order elements, finest at the undercut where the stress peaks: 0.15 mm.

  4. 4 · Solve and check

    Torsion and vibration

    CalculiX solves both. Every result is checked by hand or by a refined mesh and linked back to its feature.

The evidence

Every number comes with its check.

The checks run with every analysis and are reported, not assumed. The strength verdict stays with you: it needs your yield strength and safety factor.
