How it works

Four steps, each one a script you can rerun.

  1. 1 · Input

    Gerber and drill files

    Exactly as they go to the board house: copper, mask, silkscreen, outline, 93 plated and 6 non-plated holes.

  2. 2 · Model

    Copper, FR-4 and plating

    50 µm copper on each side, a 1.5 mm FR-4 core and 25 µm plating in every hole.

  3. 3 · Mesh

    334,889 nodes

    Layered elements through the board, connected everywhere, finest where the heat enters.

  4. 4 · Solve

    Steady state and heat-up

    CalculiX solves the temperature field: at equilibrium and second by second after power-on.

The whole story

From Gerber files to a transient thermal answer, in 77 seconds.

Assumptions: 1 W spread evenly over the 32 pads of U1, the chip itself not modelled. Convection h = 12 W/m²K on the top, bottom and edges. Temperatures are rises above ambient air. The heat-up starts with time steps of 1 ms. Board: Arduino Nano IO Shield, Gerber files from freegerber.com.
